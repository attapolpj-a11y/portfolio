LIVE COMMERCE / PROJECT MANAGEMENT

Makro PRO
Delivering 4.4 & 5.5.

Worked directly with the client and managed Makro’s 4.4 and 5.5 livestreams at Sathorn, from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m., overseeing production readiness and product information.

IN THIS PROJECT

01 / CONTEXT

15 hours. A live retail environment.

Makro’s 4.4 and 5.5 campaigns were broadcast on location at Makro Sathorn from 8 a.m. to 11 p.m.—approximately 15 hours. Equipment and connectivity had to remain ready throughout, alongside approximately 400 SKUs whose prices changed every two or three hours through a manual update process.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Keep the live moving. Keep the offer current.

Present products and promotions to support the brand’s sales, maintaining a smooth broadcast, accurate prices for each promotional window and prompt responses to customer questions.

03 / MY ROLE

From client brief to production plan.

There was no account manager or account executive handling client coordination at the time. I received the brief and worked directly with the client, translating their requirements into a clear production plan and designing the overall flow of the day-long broadcast.

04 / THINKING / PROCESS

Prepare for the points that can interrupt a live.

Unstable internet was a major risk: if the connection dropped and the broadcast ended, a new live room and product basket had to be created. I arranged rented internet with a support team available to diagnose problems and help restore the connection. We carried spare equipment for immediate replacement and positioned microphones close to the hosts’ mouths, with settings adjusted to reduce noise from the retail floor.

05 / EXECUTION

Share the workload. Respond in real time.

I shared price-update responsibilities with another producer: I handled product listings 1–200, while they handled 201–400. This helped us update prices in time for each promotional window. During the broadcast, I coordinated production and internet support when issues arose, while keeping customer responses moving.

06 / RESULT / IMPACT

Approximately THB20M across two campaigns.

The 4.4 and 5.5 campaigns generated approximately THB20 million in combined sales. With these measures in place, the livestreams ran more smoothly and the team could respond to customers promptly. I also observed more viewers joining the on-location broadcasts at Makro Sathorn.

Approximately THB20 million in combined sales from the 4.4 and 5.5 campaigns, as confirmed from my project experience.

RELATED MAKRO PROJECT / WEEKLY CHEF LIVE

Cooking first. Products in context.

Another Makro project I managed was a weekly cooking livestream with guest chefs. I took the client’s brief and SKUs, developed menu ideas for each week and prepared scripts for both the host and chef, turning the client’s requirements into a workable programme.

Product mentions followed what the chef was doing. When a particular ingredient or SKU was used, the host introduced it and explained its use. Cooking remained the main focus, preserving a natural atmosphere for both hosts and guest chefs, including chef influencers and chefs from cooking competitions.
