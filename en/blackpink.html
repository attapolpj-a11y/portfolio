FAN EXPERIENCE / CREATIVE

KBank × BLACKPINK
A Christmas gift to the fans.

Helped develop the Christmas concept and set for KBank × BLACKPINK Fan Meeting, prepared the presentation and contributed to pre-event awareness activities.

EXPERIENCE

IN THIS PROJECT

ContextObjectiveMy roleThinking / ProcessExecutionResult / Impact

01 / CONTEXT

A celebration across two countries.

KBank × BLACKPINK Fan Meeting took place during the Christmas season, connecting fans in Thailand with the artists in South Korea. Celebration and gift-giving shaped the experience as a way to thank fans for supporting BLACKPINK.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Turn appreciation into an experience.

Develop the client’s brief into a Christmas experience that thanked fans and encouraged interaction with the artists, while building awareness through activities before the fan meeting.

03 / MY ROLE

From the client’s idea to a concrete concept.

My project role was Creative. I helped turn the client’s ideas into a concrete concept and prepared the fan meeting presentation. My contributions included the Christmas and gift-themed set direction and concepts for gifts for selected participants and fans.

04 / THINKING / PROCESS

Align the vision with the budget.

The challenge was to align the client’s vision with what could be delivered within a limited budget. Before designing, I clarified the main theme, mood and elements such as gifts, Christmas trees and seasonal symbols. We refined the drafts over several rounds, sometimes rebuilding the design to better meet the brief.

05 / EXECUTION

A set the artists could play with.

The Christmas set concept I helped develop was used in the event, with gifts and props the artists could interact with. Another concept involved fans sending questions from Thailand for BLACKPINK to answer live from South Korea. Before the fan meeting, an activation at Siam during the release of Ice Cream distributed pink and green ice cream, connecting BLACKPINK and KBank’s brand colours and building awareness of the upcoming event.

06 / RESULT / IMPACT

A realised concept, with mixed feedback.

The set concept I helped develop was implemented. Positive feedback highlighted how naturally the artists interacted with the gifts, set and props. Some viewers, however, expected a more spectacular set for this special opportunity to meet their favourite artists. The project reinforced the importance of considering fan expectations alongside the client’s brief and budget constraints.

Creative responsibilities and feedback are based on my experience on this project.
