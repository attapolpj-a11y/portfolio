SPECIAL LIVE / LIVE COMMERCE

Maybelline
Listen to the audience. Guide the live.

As Producer for Maybelline Special Live, I connected promotions, stock updates and audience questions with the host’s presentation. The approximately two-hour broadcast sold more than 1,000 lipsticks.

IN THIS PROJECT

ContextObjectiveMy roleThinking / ProcessExecutionResult / Impact

01 / CONTEXT

Two hours. More than 1,000 lipsticks.

Maybelline Special Live sold more than 1,000 lipsticks during an approximately two-hour broadcast. My role on this project was Producer, before progressing to a senior role and later Project Management.

02 / OBJECTIVE

Make questions part of the buying experience.

Explain products and promotions clearly while covering audience questions, so viewers feel heard and have the information they need to decide whether to buy.

03 / MY ROLE

Keep the MC connected to the audience.

I briefed the host on promotions and stayed in communication throughout the live. I relayed stock levels so the host could update viewers and encourage purchase decisions. I also collected questions the host might miss, feeding them back and identifying the product baskets attracting audience interest at each moment.

04 / THINKING / PROCESS

Turn many comments into a clear queue.

The challenge was to address a large volume of audience questions fairly. I grouped questions by product basket and organised the presentation order, considering both earlier requests and products attracting the most interest, so the host could answer shared questions together.

05 / EXECUTION

Tell viewers what comes next.

I asked the host to tell viewers which basket was being presented, encourage interested viewers to listen to the full explanation and announce the next basket. We also explained that returning to a product could take time. This gave viewers a clear order and reassured them that their questions were in the queue. I continued relaying questions and stock updates throughout.

06 / RESULT / IMPACT

More complete answers. A strong sales result.

Organising questions and communicating the running order helped the team answer most audience comments and supported purchase decisions. The approximately two-hour Special Live sold more than 1,000 lipsticks in total.

Lipstick volume and broadcast duration are based on my confirmed project experience.
